About the trend

"APT." by ROSÉ and Bruno Mars came out in October 2024 and became one of the biggest songs in the world, with a pink-studio music video full of drum fills, leather jackets and the "apateu, apateu" chant. The AI trend recasts the video: you and a friend take ROSÉ's and Bruno's parts.

Most face swap apps paste a flat face over the clip. Genjutsu keeps the original motion, camera cuts and timing and rebuilds both performers from your photos, so every move lands on the beat.

How to make it

  1. Open this page. The APT. clip and the prompt are already loaded. You don't need to find the video or a CapCut template.
  2. Add two photos. Add one photo for ROSÉ's part and one for Bruno Mars' part. Use clear, front-facing, well-lit photos with one face each.
  3. Keep 480p. 480p is the default: it's the cheapest and looks fine on TikTok, Reels and Shorts. Pick 720p or 1080p for a sharper video.
  4. Click Generate. You'll see the exact price first. The video renders in a few minutes, and you can close the tab and come back.
  5. Download and post. Download the MP4 and post it with "APT." as the sound.
