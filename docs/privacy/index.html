Privacy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This page explains what genjutsu-trends.com ("Genjutsu Studio") collects and why. Questions or requests: support@genjutsu-trends.com.

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in and keep your credits and videos.
  • Photos and videos you upload, to make your video. They're stored privately, sent to the generation provider only through short-lived links, and deleted automatically after 7 days. We keep them that long only to look into failed videos.
  • Your videos and orders: what you made, when, the prompt text, its status, the price, and your PayPal order number and amount. For videos that fail, we also keep the provider's technical error reply, visible only to us.
  • Technical logs: like most websites, our providers (Supabase, Resend and PayPal) log technical details such as IP address and browser type when you sign in or pay.
  • Visit statistics: we count page views with GoatCounter, which uses no cookies and doesn't track you across sites. It records the page, the referring site, the country and the screen size.

Who processes it

  • Higgsfield AI generates the videos from your uploads and hosts the finished videos.
  • PayPal handles payments. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Supabase (servers in Frankfurt, Germany) stores accounts, orders and uploads.
  • Resend sends our emails (sign-in codes, video updates and occasional news).
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare serve the website, and GoatCounter counts visits.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

Uploads: 7 days. Your account, orders and video history: until you ask us to delete them. We may keep payment records longer when the law requires it.

Your choices

We may email you occasionally about new trends and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time with the link at the bottom of those emails; sign-in codes and emails about your own videos still arrive.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by emailing us from the address you signed in with.
